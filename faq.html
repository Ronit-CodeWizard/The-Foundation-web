Frequently Asked Questions

What courses do you offer?

We offer comprehensive coaching for JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET, NTSE, Olympiads, and regular schooling support for classes 4 to 12.

Where is the institute located?

Our centre is located at Durga Mahal, Pramod Nagar, Parsudih, Jamshedpur.

How can I register for a course?

You can register online through our Registration Page or visit our centre in person.

Do you provide study material?

Yes, we provide high-quality, researched study material and test series for all our competitive exam batches.
