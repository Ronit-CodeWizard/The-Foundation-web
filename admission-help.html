Admission Help

How to Apply?

Applying to The Foundation is simple. You can follow these steps:

  1. Fill the online Registration Form.
  2. Our counselor will call you within 24 hours to schedule a visit.
  3. Visit our centre for a personal counseling session.
  4. Complete the admission formalities and collect your study material.
